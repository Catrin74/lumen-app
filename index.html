Your Anchors

A small portfolio of images that hold the feeling of safety and connection — so when a harder moment pulls you away, you have somewhere to return to. Anchors are cues of who, what, where, or when you feel most grounded.

Mood Companions

For each mood, a photo of what it looks or feels like, and — when you find one — a photo of something that helps you shift or calm from it. These build up gradually, one mood at a time, whenever you land there in a check-in. There's no right or wrong, and it's not about taking a perfect picture.

Foundations

A short, one-time introduction to mindful photography — five brief lessons, at your own pace. Not part of your daily practice, just a gentle place to start if this is new to you.
